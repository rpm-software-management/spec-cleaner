# Keywords and patterns the cleaner keeps out of curly brackets, on top of
# the argument-taking macros of the vendored snapshot in macro_functions.txt
# and the macros the spec itself declares.
#
# A name the snapshot carries is not listed here: the snapshot is the
# data-driven source for argument-taking macros, and listing such a name
# again would only restate what the data already provides. Lines starting
# with '#' and empty lines are skipped by the loader.
#
# Entries with a reason recorded above them are semantic exceptions: they
# are section keywords, %files directives or parser directives rather than
# macro invocations, or plain macros taking no arguments, so the
# argument-taking snapshot cannot express why they stay unbraced.
#
# The remaining entries without a recorded reason are argument-taking
# macros the snapshot does not carry yet (whole families among them, e.g.
# the cmake_*, meson_* and yast_* helpers); they stay until the snapshot
# covers them.
